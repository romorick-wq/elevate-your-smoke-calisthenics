Your 9-minute card

Total time 9:00 exactly — warm-up, three circuit rounds, finisher, cool-down. Level 1: 20s work / 10s rest. Level 2+: 25s / 5s. Beginners scale every move.

0:00–1:00Warm-upTwo 30-second mobility / activation moves.
1:00–7:00Main circuit · 3 roundsFour movements. Level 1: 20s work / 10s rest. Level 2+: 25s work / 5s rest.
7:00–8:00FinisherSixty seconds on the pace move — steady cadence.
8:00–9:00Cool-downTwo 30-second stretches. Breathe down.

30-day progression

Calendar length is thirty days. Scheduled sessions depend on the weekly frequency you choose (3–6 days).

  • Days 1–7Find your cadence. Rest days are programmed — take them. Miss a day? Resume the next open session.
  • Days 8–14Same 9:00 structure. Form first.
  • Days 15–21Consistency beats hero days.
  • Days 21–30Final ten days step movements up one level automatically. Still nine minutes. Still scalable.

How points work

10 pts per completed session · 50 pts every 5% of the card finished · 100 pts every 5% bodyweight lost (gain = 0; capped at 4 milestones). Hours = sessions × 9 minutes. Public boards show display name (or callsign), league, sessions, streak, points, hours, and weight-loss % only — never raw lbs, phone, or organizer-only callsign when a nickname is set.

Stop for sharp pain, chest pain, faintness, severe shortness of breath, or loss of coordination. Not medical advice.
